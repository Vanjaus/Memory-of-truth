# Licensing direction

Memory of Truth is still in development. **This page explains the intended product model; it is not a software license or an offer to sell or enroll.** The engine's source code and binaries are not published in this repository.

The current plan is:

- Early development testing will be free for selected testers using their own computer or VPS and, where needed, their own third-party AI API accounts.
- Founding GitHub VIP testers will receive a permanent commercial V1 license, including V1 updates, under eligibility and test terms that will be published before enrollment.
- Other users will be able to buy a major version once a commercial release exists. Updates within that major version are intended to be included.
- A later major version may be purchased separately, with a discounted upgrade for existing owners. Owners can continue using the major version they bought, subject to its published support and compatibility terms.
- A managed cloud service, if offered in the future, will be optional and subscription based. Self-hosted use will remain part of the product plan.

## Owner control and continuity

The future beta terms and commercial license should make these principles explicit:

- A purchased major version is intended to remain usable without a continuing subscription. Updates within that major version are included. The support period for each version will be defined and published before sale; the end of support should not by itself revoke the purchased version.
- The owner-held key belongs to the user and remains under the user's control. The project must not claim ownership of that key or make surrendering it a condition for access to the user's memory.
- Ending a trial must not delete the user's memories or take away access to the owner-held key, export, or backup. Continued use of paid functions may require a purchase under the terms published for that version.
- Any later managed cloud subscription will be optional and governed by separate service terms. It will not replace a user's right to keep using a purchased self-hosted version under its published terms.

The exact beta terms, commercial EULA, support period, prices, and distribution rules have not yet been published. The engine remains private at this stage, and no particular open-source license has been selected for it. Public visibility of this documentation does not grant permission to use or distribute unreleased software.
