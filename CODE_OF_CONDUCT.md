# Community conduct

We welcome serious questions, criticism, and different views about long-term AI memory. Keep discussion focused on ideas and evidence. Treat other participants with respect, and do not harass, threaten, or disclose another person's private information.

Do not post private memories, credentials, or security vulnerabilities in public discussions. Maintainers may hide or remove posts that violate these rules and restrict repeat abuse. Use GitHub's reporting tools to flag a problem.
